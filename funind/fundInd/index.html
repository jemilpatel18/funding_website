    Got Questions?

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does FundWeb verify founders and startups? ▾
    Every company is reviewed against MCA/ROC master registry databases, director Aadhaar/PAN credentials, and historical bank statements. Verified companies earn the verified blue shield badge.
    Are startup equity investment transactions executed on checkout? ▾
    No. In compliance with SEBI and global financial regulations, actual equity investment transactions are governed by formal term sheets and licensed escrow banking rails. FundWeb checkout powers legitimate platform services (Featured listings, Pro subscriptions, pitch promotions, and KYC fast-tracks).
    How does the Razorpay platform payment engine operate? ▾
    Payments for platform services are routed through Razorpay rails with full tax invoicing (18% GST), automated commission split calculation, and instant printable payment receipts.
